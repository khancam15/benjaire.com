Design Philosophy

Built around the device. Not a trend.

Benjaire LLC was formed in 2022 to make tech accessories, starting with a case for iPhone 18. Every product starts with the device it's built for, not a mood board. Port openings machined to spec. Button travel tuned by hand. MagSafe aligned to the device it ships with.

Materials are chosen for how they perform, not how they photograph: a grip texture that holds in a pocket, an edge that won't crack after a year of use, a finish that ages instead of wearing out.

Less, but better, is the whole philosophy. Fewer products, each one held to a standard worth repeating.

How We Test

Every case goes through drop testing from typical pocket and desk heights, repeated MagSafe cycles to check magnet retention, and a fit check against the device it's built for before a batch is approved. If it doesn't pass, it doesn't ship. Questions about the process go through Contact.
