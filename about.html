Why It Exists

A Company Built
to Hold Other
Companies.

Benjaire LLC was formed in 2022 by Khaneil Campbell. It does not sell services, take clients, or ship products. It exists to start, own, and fund the brands that do.

The alternative — one personal brand stretched across consulting, writing, and products — is how most independent operators grow, and it is the thing this company was structured to avoid. A name that means everything ends up meaning nothing in particular. A buyer evaluating a consultancy and a customer buying a product are not the same person and should not land on the same page.

So each brand gets its own name, its own site, and its own audience. Benjaire holds ownership, capital, and standards behind them, and stays out of the way of the selling.

Who Runs It

Benjaire is operator-led. The founder is the person doing the work, not a layer sitting above it — decisions get made close to the product, and one person is accountable for every brand in the group.

Khaneil's professional background, certifications, and technical writing are documented on Khaneil Campbell's professional site. They are deliberately not repeated here: this page is about the company, and that one is about the person who built it.

What This Site Is Not

You won't find a service menu, pricing, a portfolio, or a booking form on benjaire.com. That material belongs to Jaire Systems, the group's technology consultancy, and moves there when it launches. If you came looking for consulting work, the Jaire Systems entry is the place to start.
