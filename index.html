Founder

Founded by
Khaneil
Campbell.

Benjaire was started in 2022 by Khaneil Campbell, a New York technologist who wanted a structure that could hold more than one idea at a time.

The decision behind this company was deliberate: rather than stretch a single personal brand across every kind of work, build a company that can stand up separate brands, each properly named and properly scoped. A consultancy should sell like a consultancy. A product line should sell like a product line. Neither should borrow the other's voice.

His technical background, certifications, and published work are documented on Khaneil Campbell's professional site. This page is about the company he built, not the résumé behind it.

Mission

"A holding company doesn't
serve customers. Its brands do."

Benjaire exists to give each brand room to be itself, with its own name, its own audience, and its own standards, backed by shared capital, shared discipline, and one founder accountable for all of it.

How We Operate
01
One Brand, One Job

Every property in the group has a single clear purpose and a single audience. We don't let them blur together, and we never reuse one brand's story to sell another.

02
Built to Stand Alone

A brand launches when it can carry its own name, its own site, and its own customers. Until then it stays listed as in development rather than announced early.

03
Operator Led

Benjaire is run by the person doing the work, not a layer sitting above it. Decisions are made close to the product, and one founder answers for every brand in the group.

04
Patient by Design

We hold what we build. Brands are measured over years rather than quarters, and we would rather launch late than ship something we'd have to walk back.
