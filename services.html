Engagement Model

How It Works

Simple, clear engagements with defined scope. You know exactly what you're getting before we start — and we move quickly once we do.

01
Intro Call

A short conversation to understand what you need, where you're headed, and which service is the right fit. No pressure — just a direct conversation.

02
Clear Scope

A straightforward proposal covering exactly what will be delivered, the timeline, and the investment. No hidden extras or vague retainers.

03
We Execute

You have one point of contact throughout. We keep you updated, move efficiently, and ask the right questions — not the obvious ones.

04
You Own It

Everything we deliver is yours — the website, resume, strategy, or system. No ongoing dependency on us unless you want it.

Standards

What We Hold Ourselves To

01
Results, Not Promises

Every engagement is scoped around what you actually get at the end — a live website, a polished resume, a clear strategy. We don't bill for conversations that don't move things forward.

02
You Keep Everything

Files, credentials, copy, strategy — it's all yours. We document what we build so you can manage it independently, with no recurring dependency on Benjaire to keep it running.

03
Honest Scope

We tell you what a project actually involves before we start. No scope creep surprises, no upsells mid-engagement. If something is outside what we can deliver well, we say so upfront.

04
Direct Communication

One point of contact throughout. We respond promptly, update you when something changes, and never make you chase us for a status. If there's an issue, you hear it from us first.
