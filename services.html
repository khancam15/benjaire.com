Engagement Model

How Engagements Work

Focused, time-bounded projects with defined deliverables. No retainers built on vague scope. Every engagement starts with a clear output and ends with something your team can operate independently.

01
Scope Call

30 minutes to understand the problem, define the right service, and confirm fit. We move fast and give you a direct answer on whether we can help.

02
Proposal

A single-page engagement document covering deliverables, timeline, and investment. No ambiguity — you know exactly what you're getting before we start.

03
Execution

We work directly with your team. Weekly updates, fast decisions, no middleman overhead. You have one point of contact throughout.

04
Handoff

Full documentation, working systems, and a team that understands how to run what we built. No dependency on Benjaire for ongoing operation.

Standards

What We Hold Ourselves To

01
Outcomes, Not Effort

Engagements are scoped to results. We do not bill for hours that do not move the project forward. If something is not working, we surface it early and adjust — not at billing time.

02
Documentation That Lasts

Every system we build ships with documentation your team can follow without us in the room. No Benjaire dependency for day-to-day operation of what we deliver.

03
Conservative Risk Profile

We flag tradeoffs clearly. We do not recommend systems we would not run ourselves. High-risk proposals get honest scoping, not optimistic projections built to win the deal.

04
Direct Communication

One point of contact. Status updates that say something real. If there is a problem, you hear about it from us directly — before it surfaces through other channels.
