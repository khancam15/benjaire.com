Two case lines, both held to the same fit and durability standard before either one ships. Join the waitlist below to hear the moment a line goes live.

Benjaire doesn't make an accessory for every device. It makes two things and holds both to one bar.

Every case is cut to the exact tolerances of the device it fits and tested for drops and wear before a batch ships. Launch dates move around that standard, not the other way around.

iPhone Cases

Slim Protection

A slim-profile case cut to the exact port, button, and camera tolerances of the current iPhone lineup. Built for MagSafe, drop-tested before any batch leaves the line.

MacBook Cases

Shell & Sleeve
Available Soon
