Common Questions
Is the $50 refundable?

The deposit is non-refundable once the project has been scoped and work has begun. If you reach out before work starts, we'll work something out on a case-by-case basis.

What services require a deposit?

Deposits are required for all web development, personal branding, and custom project engagements. Resume and career coaching sessions are booked through the scheduling page.

What happens after I pay?

You'll receive a Stripe payment receipt immediately. Khaneil will follow up within 24 hours via email to align on project scope, timeline, and the remaining balance.

Is my payment information secure?

Payments are processed by Stripe — one of the most trusted payment platforms in the world. Benjaire never stores your card details.
