The Group

OurCompanies.

Benjaire LLC holds three properties. Each one has a distinct job, a distinct audience, and its own name, and none of them borrows another's story to make a sale.

Most solo companies grow by bolting every new idea onto the same brand until the brand means nothing in particular. Benjaire was structured to avoid that.

Benjaire holds ownership, capital, and standards. The brands underneath do the actual selling. A consultancy sells like a consultancy, a product line sells like a product line. That separation is why you will not find a service menu or a booking form on this site.
