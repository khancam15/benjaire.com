Focus Areas

How We Build
the Operation

Three disciplines that work together. Channel strategy determines where we compete. Sourcing and inventory determines how reliably we can fulfill. Performance management determines whether the business is actually getting better over time.

01
Channel Strategy

We design a channel mix that balances acquisition cost, contribution margin, and operational complexity. Each channel earns its place — we cut what doesn't hold its weight and double down on what does.

Execution Note

Channel priorities are reviewed quarterly against demand shifts and margin targets, not just top-line performance.

02
Sourcing & Inventory

Supplier qualification, lead-time planning, and reorder discipline are modeled to reduce stockouts and eliminate dead inventory. We build inventory policy around real demand data, not intuition.

Execution Note

Inventory policy is set by SKU tier based on lead time, demand volatility, and target service levels — not flat rules.

03
Performance Management

We monitor contribution margin, return rates, and demand velocity to keep decisions grounded in unit economics. Weekly scorecards track what drives profitability — not what looks good in a dashboard.

Execution Note

Reporting is built around decisions, not data volume. If a metric doesn't change what we do, it doesn't go in the scorecard.

Operating Model

"Built for repeatability.
Not just reach."

We avoid growth tactics that inflate top-line numbers while weakening the business underneath. Every process is documented, measured, and improved on a defined cadence. The operations we build are designed to scale without requiring proportional increases in headcount or management overhead.

Operating Standards
01
Standardized Sourcing

Scorecards for quality, reliability, and landed cost keep supplier decisions consistent and auditable across the portfolio.

02
Forecasting Cadence

Weekly demand reviews and inventory health checks prevent reactive buying and keep working capital tied to real opportunity.

03
Exception Handling

Clear playbooks for listing quality issues, customer escalations, and fulfillment delays reduce operational drag and protect brand reputation.

04
Cash Conversion Focus

Inventory turns, payment terms, and fulfillment timing are managed together to keep cash cycling efficiently through the business.

What Good Looks Like

Measuring What Matters

Primary Target
Contribution Margin

Revenue minus variable costs. The only number that tells you whether a channel or SKU is worth running.

Fulfillment Health
On-Time Rate

Consistent delivery performance is the baseline for customer trust and repeat purchase behavior.

Inventory Quality
Sell-Through Rate

Dead inventory is a capital problem. We track turns by SKU tier to catch slow movers before they become write-offs.

Cash Efficiency
Cash Cycle Time

Days from capital outlay to cash in — the metric that determines whether growth creates or destroys working capital.
